THE BOY WHO LOVED HER IN SILENCE

Chapter 2

The Smile Behind the Shadow

Scene 1 — The Unread Message

The next morning, Kairo Shin sat beside the classroom window, holding Mika's handwritten note.

"Kairo, tum hamesha itne chup kyun rehte ho? Kabhi mujhse bhi baat kiya karo. — Mika"

He read it again. A small smile appeared on his face.

Maybe… I should talk to her today.

Suddenly, the classroom door opened.

Teacher: Students, we have a new transfer student joining us today.

A boy walked into the classroom. Black hair, sharp eyes and a confident smile.

Ren: My name is Ren Kurosawa. Nice to meet you.

But his eyes weren't looking at the teacher. They were looking at Mika.

Scene 2 — Someone From Her Past

Mika was smiling a moment ago. But when she saw Ren, her smile disappeared.

Ren: It's been a long time, Mika.

Mika: Ren…? Why are you here?

Ren: You really thought I wouldn't find you?

Mika lowered her eyes. For the first time, Kairo saw fear in her expression.

Scene 3 — The Empty Seat

Teacher: Ren, you can sit behind Kairo.

As Ren passed Mika, he whispered:

Ren: We need to talk. You haven't forgotten our promise, have you?

Mika didn't answer.

During lunch, Kairo finally gathered the courage to approach her.

Kairo: Mika… are you okay?

Mika: You… talked to me first.

Kairo: I… just wanted to ask.

Mika smiled softly. But her eyes told a different story.

Scene 4 — The Warning

After school, Kairo was walking home when Ren appeared near the school gate.

Ren: You're Kairo, right?

Kairo: Yes.

Ren: You seem interested in Mika.

Kairo remained silent.

Ren: Here's some advice. Don't get too close to her.

Kairo: Why?

Ren: Because you don't know what happened between us.

Ren walked away. Kairo stood there, unable to understand what he meant.

Final Scene — The Secret

That night, Mika sat beside her bedroom window. Her phone vibrated.

Unknown Number — 11:47 PM

"I'm back, Mika. And this time, you can't run away."

Mika's hands began to tremble.

Mika: Please… not again.

Meanwhile, Kairo opened his notebook and wrote:

"I don't know what she's hiding… but I don't want to see her cry."

CHAPTER 2 — END
