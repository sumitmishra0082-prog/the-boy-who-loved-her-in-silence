The story

A love kept quietly.

Kairo notices the little things: Mika's gentle smile, the way she helps her classmates, and how the rain catches the light around her. He has never found the courage to tell her. When they meet beneath one umbrella, silence begins to turn into a story.

The characters

Two hearts, one rainy day.

Kairo Shin

A quiet, observant student with messy black hair and a heart full of words he cannot say. He has loved Mika from afar for nearly two years.

Mika Hayashi

Warm, kind, and quietly thoughtful. Her smile makes ordinary school days feel different, even when she does not notice who is watching.
